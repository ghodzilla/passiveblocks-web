## Post 2 — Source of yield ("if you can't name who's paying, you're paying")

Every yield is someone else's cost.

Lending APY = a borrower's interest. LP fees = a trader's slippage. Farm rewards = a token being printed and sold.

If you can't name who's paying you, the answer is usually: you are — in emissions that decay or risk you didn't price.
