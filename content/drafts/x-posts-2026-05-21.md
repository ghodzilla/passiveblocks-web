## Post 1 — Withdrawal speed as the silent half of APY

Two pools, both 6% APY.

Pool A: click withdraw, hold the asset in 12 seconds.
Pool B: click withdraw, hold the asset in 90 days.

Same number on the leaderboard. Two completely different financial products.

The newsletter quotes the first half. The exit speed is the silent half.

## Post 2 — The rule that filters them

Easy filter for any new yield position:

If the APY beats open lending by >3pp, the spread is buying you something.

99% of the time, that something is your liquidity.

Either price that lock against your time horizon — or walk past it.
