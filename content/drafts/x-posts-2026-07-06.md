## Post 2 — "impermanent" loss is a marketing word (funnels /learn/impermanent-loss)

Impermanent loss becomes permanent the second you hit withdraw.

It's only "impermanent" if the pair re-converges AND you wait it out. But people withdraw exactly when it's diverged the most — panic — locking the loss in.

Call it divergence loss and you'd size LPs completely differently.
