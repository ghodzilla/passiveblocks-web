## Post 1 — compounding-frequency is a rounding error (funnels /learn/composability-risk)

Auto-compounders sell you "compounds every hour."

At 5% on $10K, hourly vs monthly compounding is worth about $1 a year.

You're routing money through 3 extra contracts to capture a rounding error — and paying it back in risk.

Optimise the rate. Not the frequency.
