## Post 1 — Mercenary liquidity / TVL mirage (funnels new /learn/mercenary-liquidity)

A $50M pool feels deep enough to always get your money out.

Then ask why the money's there. If it's farming a 20% emissions rate, it's renting — and renters stampede the day the rate drops.

The depth you trusted to exit is made of the people exiting.

## Post 2 — Slippage as the invisible tax (funnels /learn/utilisation-rate)

Gas is flat: ~$40 whether you move $1K or $100K.

Slippage isn't. In a thin pool, a $50K entry can cost 2% in and 2% out — $2,000 to rent a rate for a month.

The leaderboard quotes the APY. It never quotes the spread you cross to reach it.
