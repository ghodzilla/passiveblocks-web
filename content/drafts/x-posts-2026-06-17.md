## Post 1 — The two-chain rule (execution risk, not price risk)

Most people diversify across tokens. Almost nobody diversifies across chains.

We split the same stablecoin lending book across two chains on purpose. Same asset, same mechanism — different failure point.

A bridge exploit or a chain halt shouldn't be able to touch 100% of your book. Diversifying the rails matters as much as diversifying the bag.

## Post 2 — Lending vs LP: you can delete a whole risk category

Two ways to earn ~5% on stablecoins: lend it, or LP it.

The LP pays a bit more — until one side depegs and impermanent loss quietly eats the spread you were chasing.

Single-asset lending has no pair to come apart. A dollar in is a dollar out, plus interest. Sometimes the boring structure isn't lower yield — it's one fewer way to lose.
