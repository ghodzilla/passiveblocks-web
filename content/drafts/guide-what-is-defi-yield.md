## What "yield" actually means in DeFi

In traditional finance, yield is simple: put money in a savings account, bank lends it out, bank pays you a cut. The bank keeps most of it. You get 0.5% if you're lucky.

DeFi (decentralised finance) cuts out the bank. The same lending, borrowing, and trading activity happens — but via smart contracts on a blockchain, not a spreadsheet in a boardroom. Because there is no middleman taking a margin, the yield passed to you is higher.

That yield comes from real economic activity: borrowers paying interest, traders paying swap fees, protocols rewarding early users. When someone earns 6% on USDC in DeFi, that rate is not invented — a borrower on the other side of the transaction is paying it.

## The 3 main ways to earn on your crypto

### 1. Lending — the lowest-effort option

You deposit an asset (usually a stablecoin like USDC or USDT) into a lending protocol. Borrowers lock up collateral and borrow from your pool. You earn their interest payments, minus a small protocol fee.

**Concrete example:** $10,000 USDC deposited on Aave [AFFILIATE: AAVE] at 6% APY = $600/year, or about $50/month. You can withdraw any time. The rate floats with demand.

**Battle-tested protocols for lending:**
- **Aave** [AFFILIATE: AAVE] — the largest DeFi lending market, live since 2020, billions in TVL
- **Fluid** [AFFILIATE: FLUID] — newer, cleaner UX, competitive rates on USDC and ETH, available on Base and Arbitrum

### 2. Liquidity provision — earn trading fees

Every time someone swaps tokens on a DEX, they pay a fee — typically 0.05% to 1% of the trade. That fee goes to the people who supplied the liquidity.

**Concrete example:** $10,000 split between USDC and WETH on Uniswap V3 [AFFILIATE: UNISWAP V3]. A busy pool might generate 15–30% APY in fees during high-volatility periods. Average on a well-chosen range: roughly 10–20% annually.

The catch: **impermanent loss**. If the price of ETH moves sharply while your liquidity sits in the pool, you end up holding more of the falling asset and less of the rising one.

**Battle-tested LP protocols:**
- **Uniswap V3** [AFFILIATE: UNISWAP V3]
- **Aerodrome** [AFFILIATE: AERODROME] — leading DEX on Base
- **Orca** [AFFILIATE: ORCA] — main concentrated liquidity DEX on Solana

### 3. Liquid staking — earn while keeping flexibility

If you hold ETH, you can stake it to help secure the Ethereum network and earn ~3–4% APY. Liquid staking gives you a token (stETH, rETH) that represents your staked ETH and keeps earning — but can be traded or used elsewhere.

**Concrete example:** $10,000 in ETH staked via Lido [AFFILIATE: LIDO] earns ~3.5% APY = $350/year in ETH-denominated returns.

## The risk spectrum

| Strategy | APY range | Main risk |
|---|---|---|
| Stablecoin lending | 4–8% | Protocol exploit |
| ETH liquid staking | 3–4% | Slashing, smart contract bug |
| Stablecoin LP | 4–10% | Smart contract bug, peg break |
| ETH/BTC LP | 8–20% | Impermanent loss |
| Volatile LP with rewards | 20–60%+ | IL, token price collapse |

One rule: if the APY seems impossibly high and you cannot explain where it comes from, someone else is the yield source.
