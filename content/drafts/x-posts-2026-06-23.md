## Post 2 — drawdown asymmetry / capital preservation math

A 50% loss needs a 100% gain to break even.
A 20% loss needs 25%.
A 10% loss needs 11%.

Losses cost more to undo than to take. That's not caution talking — it's arithmetic. Protecting the downside compounds harder than chasing the upside.
