## Post 1 — "Denominated in what?" (yield-denomination risk)

A pool pays 20% APY — in its own token.

The token falls 35% that quarter. Your "gain" is now a real loss.

Always ask what the yield is paid IN. A rate paid in a volatile token is two bets stacked: the yield you wanted, and a coin you never chose to buy.
