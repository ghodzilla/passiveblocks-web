## Post 1 — The kink rule

A DeFi lending pool quoting 25% USDC APY isn't paying you 25%.

It's paying you 25% until one borrower repays.

Look at utilisation. If it's past the kink (~80–92%, varies), the rate snaps back to 5% the moment liquidity returns.
