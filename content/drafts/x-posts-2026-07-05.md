## Post 1 — cheap gas changes which strategies work (funnels new /learn/orca-solana-yield)

On Ethereum a rebalance costs ~$40, so switching pools rarely pays off.

On Solana it's a fraction of a cent.

Low fees don't just make yield cheaper — they change which strategies are even viable. Re-ranging, harvesting, moving pools all become practical. Cheap tx reward attention; they don't replace it.

## Post 2 — a full pool is a better signal than a begging one (funnels /learn/yield-mean-reversion)

The best DeFi pools often have a deposit cap. They're full. The team doesn't want the real yield diluted.

The 40% pool with room for your money? It needs you to prop up the rate.

When the good pool is closed and the amazing one is begging, believe the closed one.
