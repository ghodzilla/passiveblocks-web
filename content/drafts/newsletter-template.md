## Structure (every issue)
1. **Header** — logo + issue number/date
2. **Hook** — 1-2 punchy lines on the week's biggest move (ETH price, TVL, rate milestone)
3. **ETH price card** — live ETH price + 7d % change
4. **This Week in Crypto** — 4 news items, each with protocol logo + 2-line take + chart if applicable
5. **DeFi Deep Dive** — 200w educational section, one concept per issue, visual (utilisation bar, chart, etc)
6. **Top Yield Picks** — 3 picks in card format: protocol, chain, APY (big green number). Single-asset only.
7. **Intelligence Signals** — 4 signals from on-chain data (whale flows, inflows, upcoming catalysts, stablecoin supply)
8. **Tool of the Week** — one affiliate tool per issue (Ledger issue 2, rotate Trezor once approved)
9. **Sign-off** — "forward to someone on 2.8% USDC"
