## Post 1 — Slippage as the invisible tax (funnels new /learn/slippage-tax)

Gas is flat: ~$40 whether you move $1K or $1M.

Slippage isn't. Push $50K through a thin pool and you pay ~2% in AND ~2% out — $2,000 to rent a rate for a month.

The leaderboard quotes the 14%. It never quotes the spread you cross to collect it.

## Post 2 — The break-even horizon nobody calculates (fresh angle)

A 14% pool vs a 6% pool isn't an 8-point upgrade.

Costs to switch (gas + slippage + a taxable event) are paid once, up front. The extra yield drips in daily.

So the real question isn't "is 14% bigger" — it's "will I still be in this pool when it finally pays for the move?"

Usually: no.
