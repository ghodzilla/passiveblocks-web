## Post 1 — a depeg is a liquidity event, not always a solvency one (funnels /learn/stablecoin-collateral-types)

March 2023: USDC "depegged" to 88c. Anyone who could wait lost $0 — Circle redeemed at $1 the whole time. That 12c was paid entirely by forced sellers who panicked. A depeg isn't always solvency. Often it's liquidity. Know which one you're in before you sell.

## Post 2 — real yield = nominal − token supply inflation (funnels /learn/yield-denomination-risk)

"20% APY" paid in a token whose supply inflates 30% a year isn't 20%. The new coins minted to pay you dilute the ones you already hold. Real yield = nominal − supply inflation. Ask two things: what's it paid in, and how many more get printed while I hold?

**Reply (link):** What is your yield actually paid IN? → https://passiveblocks.io/learn/yield-denomination-risk
