## Post 1 — Fee APR = volume ÷ TVL (LP-quality metric) — FRESH

An LP gets paid for volume, not size.

A $500M pool doing $3M/day pays each dollar less than a $40M pool doing $40M/day.

Big TVL feels safe — it also splits the fees thinner.

The LP number that matters isn't TVL. It's volume ÷ TVL: how hard each dollar works.

## Post 2 — The dust trap: when claiming costs more than the yield — FRESH

You farmed $6 of reward tokens this week.

Claiming costs $4 gas. Selling them, $3 more.

You'd pay $7 to collect $6.

Reward farms quietly keep the yield too small to claim — the smaller your bag, the more they keep. Auto-compounding lending never charges you to get paid.
