## Post 1 — Stable LP vs lending honest math

Stable LP on USDC/USDT: ~6.5% APY.
USDC lending on Fluid: ~4.6% APY.

The 1.9pp gap is the price of depeg risk you can't hedge.

In March '23, USDC depegged to $0.88 for 72 hours. Lenders: unchanged. Stable LPs: ate a year of yield in one weekend.

On <$50K, lending wins.

## Post 2 — Withdrawal speed is half of yield quality

The yield number nobody compares:

Aave USDC: withdraw in one tx. Settled in 12 seconds.
Pendle PT-USDC: locked until maturity (avg ~90 days).
Liquid staking: unstake queue of 1–8 days depending on chain.

A 1% APY gap means nothing if you can't get out before the protocol bleeds.
