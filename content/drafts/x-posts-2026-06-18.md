## Post 1 — "Annualised ≠ what you'll earn"

A pool quoting 40% APY that's existed for 3 weeks is annualising 3 weeks of emissions into a yearly costume.

APY assumes today repeats for 365 days. Emissions don't. By the time you've bridged in, the rate that lured you is already decaying.

Ask how old the number is.

## Post 2 — "Gas is a % of your bag, and it's brutal on small ones"

A $40 rebalance is 4% of a $1,000 position. On $100,000 it's 0.04%.

Same trade. 100x the drag on the small wallet.

So the counterintuitive rule: the less you have, the LESS you should chase yield. Friction eats small accounts alive.
