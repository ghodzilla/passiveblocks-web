## Post 1 — Utilisation as the APY tell

A lending pool quoting 22% APY on stablecoins almost always shares one feature: 98%+ utilisation.

That isn't a high-yield pool.

That's a pool paying you to fund the next lender's exit, because the current ones can't get out.

Two clicks deeper on the dashboard is free.

## Post 2 — Same protocol, same borrow rate, different APY

Two USDC pools. Same protocol. Same 8% borrow rate.

Pool A: 80% utilised → lenders earn 6.4%.
Pool B: 30% utilised → lenders earn 2.4%.

The "yield" wasn't in the APY column. It was in the utilisation column the whole time.
